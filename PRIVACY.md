# Hardware Lens — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Hardware Lens shows what your phone's hardware can sense. It is built so that **nothing about you leaves your phone**.

## What the app collects
**Nothing.** Hardware Lens has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no servers.

## What the app reads, and why
All of this is read and shown on your screen only. None of it is stored (except NFC history, below) or sent anywhere.

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Location (precise) | Android requires it to list Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth devices, cell towers and GPS satellites. Your position is shown on the GPS screen only. |
| Nearby devices / Bluetooth | Scanning for nearby Bluetooth devices. |
| Nearby Wi-Fi devices | Listing Wi-Fi networks on Android 13+. |
| Physical activity | The step counter on the Sensors → Tools screen. |
| NFC | Reading cards and tags you tap against the phone. |
| Internet / network state | Finding devices on your own Wi-Fi network, and the optional update check. |

## Data stored on your phone
- **NFC history:** your last 50 NFC scans are saved in the app's private storage so you can look at them again. You can delete them any time in Settings → Clear NFC history, or by uninstalling the app.
- **Settings:** your theme, units and similar choices.
- **CSV exports:** sensor recordings you choose to share are written to the app's cache and handed to the app you pick.

## Payment cards
When you tap a bank card, Hardware Lens reads only the card's public payment directory (which payment networks it supports). It never asks the card for its number, expiry date, or transaction history.

## Network requests
- **Device discovery** talks only to devices on your local network.
- **Update check** (GitHub builds only, can be turned off in Settings) downloads a small `version.json` file from github.com. No identifier is sent; GitHub sees an ordinary web request.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
